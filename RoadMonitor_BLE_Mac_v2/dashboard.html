Setup, thresholds & connection help

Use Chrome at http://localhost:8000/dashboard.html. Turn on Bluetooth and allow Chrome in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth. Connect from this page; pairing in macOS settings is unnecessary.

If the chooser is empty: reset the board, attach its external antenna, close other BLE apps, and use the button below. Serial Monitor at 115200 should show “BLE advertising: RoadMonitor-S3”.

After connecting, hold the mounted sensors still over flat ground and click Calibrate. A three-second calibration is required after every reset or mounting change. Use a stable downward-facing mounting about 10–60 cm above a matte surface for the initial bench test.

Defaults are experimental, not validated detection thresholds. Surface changes need two consecutive readings. Ice requires a separate sensing and validation approach. No TI radar is connected by this firmware.

Offline mode makes no external requests. Enabling street maps sends tile requests for the displayed area to OpenStreetMap. With cloud off, your route stays in this tab unless exported. Cloud recording saves readings on this Mac and uploads them to your Firebase account. Export before refreshing.
